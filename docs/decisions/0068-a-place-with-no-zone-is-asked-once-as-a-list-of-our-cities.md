# 0068. A place that matches no Zone is asked once as a list of our cities; None of these is the Zone Knockout

- Status: Accepted, refined by 0076
- Date: 2026-10-12
- Refines: 0042 (a Zone Knockout needs a recorded Zone), 0037 (the pending choice), 0043 (the backend records the tap), 0044 (the privacy notice link is guaranteed like the AI disclosure), 0062 (the city offer) and 0012 (an automated Knockout rests on code)

## Context
In a demo chat on real Haiku (2026-10-12, Application `app_01M4PGC8A4JPQ3NAEK0KK03EWA`, an MX web chat in English) the Candidate wrote "I live in Texas!" and then "I live in the united states". The agent answered "I couldn't find Texas... where you live in Mexico?" and "We only hire delivery drivers in Spain and Mexico, so I need a neighbourhood and city there...". The record shows two `answer_invalid` events (`zone`, `unresolved`), no Knockout, and a loop.

The cause is in code, not in the model. The Zone Knockout (0042) fires for a recorded Zone that no Service Area covers. A place that matches no Zone at all (Texas) is only an unresolved answer: a miss, then another, then an Escalation. The agent also has no approved list of where Grupo Sazón hires, so it improvises, and the one thing it knows (Spain and Mexico) is not what a Candidate needs to hear. Separately, because this Candidate opened with his name, the agent's first reply had the AI disclosure (0044) but no link to the privacy notice.

## Decision
- **The backend gives the agent the only list of areas it may name.** `service_cities` is the cities with at least one Service Area in the Application's country, derived from the reference tables (a Zone covered by an active Location's Service Area; `criba.tools.funnel.service_cities`), never typed. `search_zones` returns it when nothing matched and the funnel is not on offer (the funnel's own question names the same cities). `get_application` does not: the first real-model run (2026-10-12) with the list in the record had Haiku judge "Texas" by itself, answer "we only hire in Spain and Mexico, so Texas is outside our area" and never search, so no funnel was offered. The agent names no other area and never a Location's address before booking. A Candidate clearly in neither served country may be told in one line that we hire in Spain and Mexico (a line in the Screener skill).
- **The funnel asks once.** When the Candidate's place matches no Zone (`search_zones` finds nothing, or the agent records an unresolved Zone, `null`), the backend asks, in a fixed question in the Application's language (`channel/copy.py`, SYNTHETIC): "We hire delivery drivers in these cities: ... Can you work in one of them?" The pending choice (0037) is one option per city plus "None of these" / "Ninguna de estas", as a list. It is an `answer_ambiguous` event with `funnel: true`, the options and the Candidate's message id: ids and codes only (0035). Once per Application; asking again for the same Candidate message (the agent searched, then recorded `null`, in one turn) returns the same question and writes nothing; it counts no miss.
- **A city tap leads to that city's Zones.** The backend records the tap before the agent runs (0043) as the city's Zones in `record_answer`: buttons for 2 or 3, a list for 4 to 10, a text re-ask for the neighbourhood above 10 (0062's offer, 0042's rules). The agent receives the result.
- **"None of these" is the Zone Knockout, in code.** A `null` Zone answer while the funnel is the live choice, asked for an earlier Candidate message, closes the Application with the Zone criterion (`outside_service_areas`) and returns the approved Knockout template at once, with the requirements link and the "revisión" path, exactly as an uncovered Zone does (0016). The tap is that `null` (`city_none`). A second typed answer after the funnel that still matches no Zone, which the agent records as `null`, is the same Knockout. No Zone is stored: `zone_input` keeps the Candidate's own latest typed words (a tap's text is the label) for a Review, and the Application detail marks the Zone as the failed check. A Reopen after the Knockout (0033) works as before, on a valid new Zone.
- **A typo or a known city never knocks out.** A typo is fuzzy-matched to its Zone, a known city is 0062's list, a `null` after a city list is an ordinary miss, and the funnel is offered once. The Knockout rests on the Candidate's tap or second answer, and on whether any Zone matched. There is no word list against the Candidate's text (0042).
- **When the funnel is offered.** The Application is open, under agent control, in Screening, and the Zone is the next field or a correction at the confirm step (0048); no Zone confirmation is waiting for its Sí or No; the search carries no `country` filter; the last message was not spoken and the channel is not `voice` (a Candidate on a call cannot tap a list); and the country has between 1 and 9 cities (a list holds ten rows, one of them "none of these").
- **The question reaches the Candidate whatever the agent writes.** The tool result carries `message` (the question), and the Screener skill says to send it. The router also checks, as for the booking link (0064): when this run offered the funnel and its options are the pending choice, a reply without the question is replaced by it, and a run with no output sends it.
- **A one-line note rides with the typed answer.** Real Haiku, in 3 of its first 6 Texas conversations, judged "Texas" or "the united states" itself ("we only hire in Spain and Mexico, so Texas is outside our area") and answered in prose without calling a tool, so no funnel was offered and no `null` was ever recorded. When the router runs a typed message while the Zone is the next field it puts a `[zone]` line ahead of the Candidate's text in the run input: "look it up with search_zones before you reply, whatever the place", or, while the cities question is open for an earlier message, "with no match call record_answer(zone, null) and send what it returns; a question gets an answer in words". It is written from the record only (the next field, whether the funnel is the pending choice), never from the Candidate's words. A tap, a system turn and a spoken turn get none. `[zone]` is a control marker: stripped from Candidate text, like the others. The Screener skill says the line is the backend's, as it does for `[criba date]`.
- **The privacy notice link is guaranteed in the first message,** extending 0044. `with_privacy_notice` appends one line with the link (in the Application's language) to the first agent message of each Application that has not carried it; said once; never on a system turn, a voice Application or a spoken turn. It runs after the AI disclosure check.

## Alternatives considered
- **Knock out at the first place that matches no Zone:** "Texas" can be a Candidate who will move, a typo the ranker missed, or a model that failed to search, and the Candidate would never be asked.
- **Recognise a place outside Spain and Mexico in code (a country or state word list):** the word list 0042 removed, and the first unlisted country would loop again.
- **Put the cities in the skill:** a second copy of the seed that goes stale, and the skill is at its size cap.
- **Offer the funnel in `search_zones` only, or in `record_answer` only:** the agent sometimes searches and never records (0062), and sometimes records `null` without searching.
- **Let `search_zones` write the Knockout:** a read-only lookup that can end an Application, on a query the agent may be only exploring.
- **Add `knockout_zone` wording for "no area at all":** the Zone template names the Zone and falls back to "your area" / "tu zona", which is true here.
- **Prepend the privacy sentence to every first message:** the same link twice in the usual case, when the agent already links it.

## Consequences
- The demo chat's first reply would have been the cities question with a list; "None of these" or "the united states" a second time ends in the Zone Knockout with the template and the review path.
- A Zone Knockout can now have no Zone value: `zone_id` is null, `zone_input` holds the words, and `answer_status` says `knockout`. Reviews, Reopen and the summary read it as before.
- **Not on voice (known gap):** a Voice call gets no funnel; the agent may name `service_cities` aloud and an unresolved Zone is a miss as before. The Voice Screener skill is unchanged.
- **Contract** (additive, for the merge notes): `tools.json` `search_zones_output.message` and `service_cities` (and `options_attached` also when nothing matched); `tool_error.message` and the `zone_funnel` reason (not counted). `api.openapi.yaml` `EventData_answer_ambiguous.funnel`; `src/web/src/api/schema.d.ts` was patched with that hunk only (openapi-typescript 7.13.0). No migration.
- The Screener skill gains three sentences and loses the same number of characters elsewhere (13,187 of 13,200; the cuts are listed in the build report). The run input gains one line of about 30 tokens on the typed turns of the Zone question.
- Existing tests that compare an agent's first message to the fake's reply now expect the notice line (`first_message` in `tests/channel/conftest.py`); a `null` Zone that was a counted miss is now the funnel's first question (`test_record_answer.py`, two assertions).
- Real-model evidence (Haiku 5.5, own stack, 2026-10-12, $0.099 in all): with the cities in `get_application` the first Texas run failed (no search, an answer in prose); without them the next Texas runs passed. The typed second answer looped for 60 messages without the `[zone]` note (one flow, $0.04) and passed with it. The final build passed 7 of 7 flows: Texas three times, the city tap twice, the Tepito list and the no-licence Knockout. Known gap: a model that ignores the `[zone]` note can still answer in prose; then no funnel is offered and the Knockout waits for a search or a `null`, as it did before this decision.
- Tests: `tests/tools/test_zone_funnel.py`, `tests/channel/test_zone_funnel_reply.py`, `tests/channel/test_copy.py`, `tests/api/test_applications.py`; the sim persona `zone_texas_funnel_en` (the fake chat and the stub model play the funnel); the live flows `zone_funnel_none` and `zone_funnel_city`.
